Black Shell Editor

This is the simple control panel for the website. Click a section below. Change a field, hit Save, and the public site updates.

  • Site text — headlines, phone, email, about copy, everything written.
  • Home page cards / Services / Jobs — the repeating lists.
  • Projects — add a job with a photo. Extra photos go in the gallery on that project.
  • Journal posts — optional news. Unpublished posts stay hidden.
  • Contact form inquiries — messages visitors send. They also email you.

Tip: one bullet per line in the bullet-list boxes. Leave a photo empty and a placeholder is used.
